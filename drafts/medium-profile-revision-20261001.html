Vitalii Oborskyi — Delivery Leadership, AI Governance & Architecture

I work at the intersection of software delivery, AI architecture and the operating models around engineering systems.

My background spans more than 20 years in IT: quality engineering, project management, PMO and delivery leadership. My practical work has included building delivery functions, resource planning, risk governance and organizational design. That experience shapes the questions I research: what changes when teams use AI to produce software, and what changes when model judgment becomes part of the product itself?

Two connected research directions

Uncertainty Architecture is an evolving, openly developed draft specification and research project on designing and operating systems whose consequential runtime responsibilities depend partly on probabilistic Model Judgment. Its engineering vocabulary includes Thinking Systems, Judgment Nodes, Uncertainty Boundaries and the AI Control Plane. The aim is to make boundaries, evidence, authority and corrective action explicit. It is not a claim of certification, an accepted industry standard or guaranteed safe behavior.

The Subprime Code Crisis examines AI-assisted software delivery through evidence-governed research synthesis. It asks how code generation changes verification work, system understanding and long-term ownership. The research distinguishes sources, interpretations and limitations rather than treating every observed result as a universal productivity claim.

Writing and audiences

I write for architects, engineering leaders, product owners, delivery managers and PMO practitioners. My topics include AI governance and control theory, LLM application architecture, software verification, socio-technical systems and human–AI operating models.

Selected work includes Uncertainty Architecture: Why AI Governance is Actually Control Theory, Uncertainty Architecture: Thinking Systems, When the Controlled Object Changes, and AI, the Externalization of Reasoning, and the Verification Crisis. Ukrainian editions are published on DOU; more articles are available through my Medium profile.
