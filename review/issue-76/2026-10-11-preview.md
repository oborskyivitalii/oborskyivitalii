landmark stays at the lower right and the shared line chart stays at the upper
left on every page. This is valid feedback against AC04's visible-composition
intent. The previous five parameter sets changed centers and depths enough for
the model test, but the root-to-symbol assignment stayed fixed and kept these
recognisable landmarks in the same screen regions. The earlier automated pass
was narrower than the visual requirement, and the first preview is historical
evidence rather than an accepted AC04 visual result.
