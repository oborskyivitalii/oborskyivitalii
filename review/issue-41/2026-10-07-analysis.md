## SEO and implementation guardrails

1. Preserve the useful topical structure and existing URLs/fragments. Keep all
   index links in ordinary generated `<a href>` HTML, available without JS.
   Native disclosures may reduce visual density; do not fetch sources on expand,
   hide a keyword dump or make the catalog depend on a LinkedIn/Reddit embed.
2. An **editorial primary URL** selects where the button goes. It is not a
   declaration that Google treats that platform as canonical. Canonical hints,
   redirects and sitemaps have distinct effects; Google can select a different
   canonical. The site's writing index is a collection page and must not declare
   a Medium or LinkedIn article its own canonical.
3. Preserve authored page titles/descriptions and topic vocabulary except for
   documented copy corrections. Use names and roles naturally in Research;
   do not pack famous names/employers into every title or description. Outgoing
   evidence links make claims inspectable; they do not promise rankings or
   transfer those people's institutional authority to the author.
4. Keep `Person.sameAs` restricted to the author's identity profiles. Advisors,
   articles, citations and discussion threads are different entities. Retain
   `CollectionPage`/`ItemList`; calculate its primary count and positions from
   the visible records. Do not emit Review/AggregateRating, fake endorsement,
   employer affiliations, invented publication dates or unverified translations.
   No extra schema relationship is required merely because a link exists.
5. Alternate editions must keep their own metadata. Do not overwrite a
   publisher date with an earlier LinkedIn date or treat `dateModified` as
   publication. A same-topic or translated article is not automatically `sameAs`.
   If later article-level structured data is needed, review the actual version
   relationship first and keep markup consistent with visible content.
6. This increment does not republish bodies or change external canonicals.
   A later owned-article migration follows #5/#7. Cross-platform canonical control
   must be actually observed before promising consolidation; do not assume a
   LinkedIn setting exists. Domain/indexing/absolute identity work remains
   #39/#8, based on their then-current merged source. Staging stays non-indexable.
7. Content adds no runtime packages, extra animation loops, remote widgets or
   telemetry. New section heights require existing native-scroll/anchor/filter
   reflow verification; avoid altering scene paths just to fit a paragraph.
8. Evaluate discovery after production with #11/#8 Search Console queries/pages,
   impressions/clicks and available referral measurements. There is no measured
   ranking uplift or demand forecast for this content change.

Primary guidance checked 2026-10-07:
[canonicalization](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/consolidate-duplicate-urls),
[structured-data fidelity](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/sd-policies),
[crawlable links](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/links-crawlable),
[sameAs](https://schema.org/sameAs), [subjectOf](https://schema.org/subjectOf).
These support the guardrails; the page-selection and density choices are editorial
recommendations, not Google ranking rules.
