**Method and source checks**

The plan appropriately retains unknown exact window/refresh/language and avoids the provider's stronger marketing inferences about traffic and easy opportunities.

The saved sample's job/certification/tutorial noise, unrelated AI ROI strings and standalone continuous-evaluation ambiguity are real in the raw replies. The plan's narrower vocabulary treatment is justified.
- [Google AI-search guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) supports ordinary crawlable content, useful visible text, matching structured data, and no special AI file/schema.
