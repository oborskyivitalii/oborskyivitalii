## Substantive conclusions

**Content and audience.** The two buyer situations, three bounded offers,
seven-section Home, research bridge and contact route preserve the maintainer's
intent. The original Executive Brief qualitatively supports that offer scope;
private PDF contents and unverified numerical achievements need not be published.
All five selected English editions, the secondary LinkedIn rendition and all eight
public-discussion entries were freshly checked against their originals by the
content reviewer. The proposed wording describes exact public actions, without
turning them into clients, institutional endorsement or research validation.

**Research meaning.** The plan keeps UA and Subprime as bounded author/research
theses and links their current owners. The selected source materials and current
upstream definitions support the proposed explanation. It does not need older
universal claims, a predetermined bottleneck diagnosis, a guarantee of stable
control or a new canonical technical model. The decorative scene is not evidence
for either research program. The review was a bounded meaning/source-use check,
not validation of all upstream research.

**Search evidence.** Independent recomputation confirmed five provider responses,
25 country/phrase results for 20 phrases, 17 positive estimates and eight unknowns;
56 autocomplete replies contain 492 suggestion occurrences and 256 unique strings.
Raw and normalized records agree, apart from the corrected unsupported currency
label. The original 48-query inventory contains hypotheses, not 48 measured
queries. Vocabulary evidence does not identify qualified buyers, select a business
territory, establish ranking opportunity or predict leads. The strategy reasonably
keeps broad high-volume terms from displacing the agreed offer. No new free-tool
quota, signup or paid data was needed for these corrections.

**Execution and visual direction.** The small Canvas-based scene, shared geometry,
facets and scroll/topic interaction are feasible within the existing static site.
The rendered planning SVGs communicate a useful direction; they do not prove the
actual portrait composition, mobile contrast or motion quality. The revised stages
cover the material interaction, archive, fallback and export risks. Actual desktop/
mobile Day/Night, keyboard, no-JS, Off/reduced, history, print and idle observations
remain acceptance work for S2–S4.
