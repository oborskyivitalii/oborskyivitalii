## Source/argument evidence and limits
This is a **conditional systems-engineering hypothesis**, not a research-established prediction that the industry cannot ever find a universal balance. The comparison with different types of systems and customers is reasoned illustration, not measured population prevalence. DORA 2025 documents organizational-context variation but **does not establish impossibility of universal convergence**; the article's own [DORA 2026 note](https://dora.dev/insights/balancing-ai-tensions/) and risk-management references frame bounded trade-offs. The CTO choice is a normative decision test, not a claim of experimental proof.
