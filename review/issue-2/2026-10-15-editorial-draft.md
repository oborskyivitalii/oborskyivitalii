### Research intake before the revision

Public primary inputs inspected on15October:

- Anthropic, *Bringing Code Review to Claude Code*,9March2026: vendor reports
  increased output and a review bottleneck, but explicitly retains human PR
  approval. Do not turn review-comment counts into demonstrated defect prevention.
- Anthropic, *The AI-native SDLC playbook*,21August2026: advocates changing the
  whole lifecycle and retaining control objectives with new enforcement. Include
  this counterweight; do not falsely say vendors ignore rebalancing altogether.
- Microsoft/Jay Parikh, *Introducing Command Line, and the new rules for builders*,
  2June2026: review bottleneck and disposable-code headings, but scopes durable
  specs to bounded features, retains architectural oversight and prioritisation,
  and says the durability of its proposed rules remains to be seen.
- GitHub/Den Delimarsky, Spec Kit introduction,2September2025: spec-centred agent
  workflow, small changes and human verification; described as an experiment.
- Cognition, DeepWiki introduction,5May2025: documentation and search to help
  developers understand repositories. Not evidence of safe replacement of
  engineering understanding.
- Anthropic, *Redesigning source control at Anthropic*,13October2026: Scion/
  Antique internal engineering case. Reported2500→over30000 daily merges,
  checkout two-orders-of-magnitude speedup,200x lower infrastructure CPU/memory
  footprint versus its old mirrors. These are distinct metrics, not end-to-end
  delivery/user-value multipliers. Unusual workload explicitly acknowledged.
  Engineers retain design understanding and every-change intent review, with
  simulations/fuzzing/access controls; not a universal Git-replacement prescription.
- Thompson/Hamilton/Rust, *Feature Fatigue*,2005: publisher metadata plus first
  author's university-hosted dissertation essay inspected. Three product studies
  distinguish capability expectations from usability/satisfaction in use. No
  modern SaaS release-rate threshold or universal human cognitive-speed bound.
- Kohavi et al., *Online Experimentation at Microsoft*,2009: original Microsoft
  research abstract supports testing feature effects rather than assuming sound
  advance prioritisation. No unverified percentage of failed ideas imported.
