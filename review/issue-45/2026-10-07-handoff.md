## Intent and findings

Make the existing world formula crisp and give large heading glyphs a visible
reading margin. Keep the exact artwork, colors, tilt, pulse and camera motion.
The supplied marked screenshots were inspected; private browser/notification
chrome is excluded from the repository.

| Finding | Source | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rear formula planes are separated farther than the glyph stroke and use extra translucent paint, producing displaced pale contours. | `site/scenes/world.cjs`, `site/engine/renderer.cjs` | Reduce formula-only extrusion from 0.32 to 0.10 world units; use the shared world haze alpha for all three planes and explicit high-quality image sampling. |
| Inline title background ends at each text fragment's edge. | `site/engine/styles.css` | Add a zero-blur 0.16em paint spread to each native cloned title fragment. |
| Standalone large headings and Writing years need the same reading margin. | Shared CSS and authored Color reading styles | Extend local solid mask cores before the existing feather; preserve element sizes and line boxes. |

and at most 24 triangle submissions per formula paint. Global Canvas resolution,
the shared clock and all other scene geometry are unchanged. Static/no-Canvas
SVG derives from the same artwork and corrected extrusion/opacity. This issue
