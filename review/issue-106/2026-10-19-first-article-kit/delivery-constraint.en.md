A team generates, reviews and integrates eight comparable changes a week.

Now suppose generation capacity rises to one hundred. Review and integration stay at eight.

Admit every change: delivery stays at eight, while unfinished work grows by ninety-two a week. Control intake: the queue need not grow, but the downstream stages still cap delivery.

This is a synthetic fixed-capacity illustration, not a measured AI outcome or a diagnosis of every team's bottleneck.

The useful question is what changed across the whole delivery system - including understanding, quality, recovery and useful outcomes.
