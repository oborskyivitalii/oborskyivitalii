## First finding — native footer points at an offline-only route form

**F1 — P2 product defect; blocking the required native navigation/runtime gate.** At both1440×900 and390×844, after Writing → R9 article → browser Back, the actual visible footer anchor has href `http://127.0.0.1:36395/writing.html?view=talks` and label “Keep scrolling for Talks ↓”. Its query target agrees with the real `SiteNavigation.primaryRoutes` order. Playwright performs a successful visible/enabled/stable native click. The URL becomes `writing.html?view=talks`, but the page remains Writing and jumps to its introduction. No new camera or route-block transition begins.

Exact source mechanism at commit `3c532d2f23e9568e5d88b9b50139d45a4867829e`:

- `site/effects/flight.cjs:164` always creates `hint.href = '?view=' + route`.
- `site/engine/navigation.js:275–306` interprets `view` only in the embedded document branch. Native route selection uses the pathname, so `/writing.html?view=talks` resolves to Writing.
- `site/engine/navigation.js:1227–1240` handles that as a same-page link, pushes its changed URL and calls `restoreScroll(0,0)`.

This chain agrees with both failure screenshots and the raw mobile trace. Trace `call@711` resolves the real `<a href="?view=talks" class="scroll-continue">`, performs its click at41044.239ms and completes at41070.096ms. Its after snapshot at41075.566ms has frame URL `http://127.0.0.1:36395/writing.html?view=talks`. The wait for Talks lasts through51084.206ms; the captured screen still shows Writing. The trace and report contain camera plans only for navigation1 Writing→article and navigation2 article→Writing. There is no footer-next camera plan or departure after navigation2.

Treatment: correct the product's footer link through the existing native/offline destination contract and add a focused regression for the actual anchor in both modes. Rewalk the affected bounded charter on the corrected source, including the currently unobserved footer Back and second cycle. Preserve this failed campaign. Do not loosen the destination assertion, increase its timeout or treat a URL/query change as arrival.
