“Code review is the next bottleneck” is a useful observation. It is not the full diagnosis.

My new article develops the problem statement behind my DevCon talk: AI adoption has pushed the SDLC out of balance at industry level.

The question is not only whether machines can check code faster. It is whether the organisation can still understand, integrate, operate and own what it accepts into its products.

I use code review to make the distinction concrete: one practice can detect defects, transfer knowledge and help a team become capable of recovering a system under pressure. Automating one function does not automatically preserve the others.

The article includes three diagrams, links to the evidence and the limits of what that evidence establishes. It ends with a way to frame a bounded investigation—not another universal AI recipe.
