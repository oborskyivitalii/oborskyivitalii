## Reproduction and treatment

camera is already at its exact closing pose at 80%, unchanged at 90/95/100%.
At 390×844, range is 7690px and the camera is already finished at 70%.
The shared mapper uses the last heading's top as its endpoint. Home also repeats
the closing pose in two trailing sections. Writing measures an archive-row edge
instead of the complete native range; a one-record filter needs a valid short path.

`fitScrollStops` keeps increasing semantic interior waypoints, coalesces repeated
poses and binds first/last to 0/current maximum scroll. Writing keeps its early
introduction, topic paths and anchored reflow, but its endpoint is the real bottom.
Former endpoint anchors cannot create an interior plateau after content growth;
short single-record results still have a continuous path. Empty archives retain
their still camera and ambient cycle. No spacer or scroll interception is added.

The actual autonomous-file probe additionally found temporary content transforms
at animated arrival could leave a stale heading/range measurement. Navigation now
refreshes the mapping after the arrival transform is removed. The maintained
navigation matrix requires exact post-arrival endpoint/final-gesture evidence on
Research, Writing, Talks and Credits; every offline entry is probed as well.

Main/body resize, main child/text/visibility edits, font loading, viewport changes
and route mounts recalculate the path. Enabled motion follows the new mapping;

## Regression contract

- Pure endpoint and Writing reflow tests run in standalone runtime CI and the
  complete PR/release build suite.
- Every normal functional case, all five routes/engines/themes/desktop/mobile,
  checks original content, appended block, resized block, enlarged footer,
  same-height reorder, restoration and changed viewport. Writing additionally
  checks one-record filtering; a short-content fixture confirms zero added scroll.
- Each fixture samples 90/95/99/100% and must move through every final gesture,
  reach the configured exact final pose only at the real bottom, then return to
  the exact opening pose. Observers update automatically; fixtures dispatch no
  synthetic resize to hide missing content observation.
- A same-total-height reorder additionally probes the actual semantic waypoint
  against its newly measured position. Expected geometry uses the native rounded
  scroll offset, with a strict 0.0001 world-distance bound; an arbitrary tolerance
  must not mask stale marker positions. The aggregate recomputes its distance.
- The strict aggregate requires this evidence. Missing cases/fields, an observed
  final plateau, an incorrect bottom offset or missing filter evidence reject
  controlled aggregate fixtures. Content-only changes receive the same checks;
  no job, budget or publication guard is relaxed.
