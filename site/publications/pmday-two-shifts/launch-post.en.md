AI changes the delivery system and the product itself.

When AI helps us generate software, we need to check the complete delivery flow: review, integration, team understanding and recovery. Faster generation may move the constraint; it does not automatically increase useful releases.

When a running product depends on model judgment, we need to specify acceptable behavior, gather decision-ready evidence and make permissions, fallback and intervention work under real operating conditions.

My PMDay talk connects these two changes through requirements, verification, release and operational responsibility. The access-recovery assistant is an illustrative design example, not a deployed case study.

A practical first step: choose one workflow, agree its useful outcome and limits, identify the evidence and decision owners, and rehearse the fallback.
