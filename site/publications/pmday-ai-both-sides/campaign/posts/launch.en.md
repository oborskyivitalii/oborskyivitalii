We can delegate more work. We still have to own the consequences.

AI changes software engineering in two connected ways: it changes how we build software, and it lets the products we build exercise judgment at runtime.

The first can move work and risk into review, integration, maintenance and recovery. The second makes authority, evidence, fallback and real human capacity part of the product's operating design.

Neither shift comes with a finished recipe. Promising gains deserve serious attention - and a baseline that tells us whether they hold in our own system.

The Sum of Our Technology develops that argument from my UA Online PMDay 2026 Autumn talk: what must remain dependable around the capabilities we delegate?
