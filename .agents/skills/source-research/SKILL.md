# Source research

1. Restate the audience's question and separate observed facts from the
   explanation or recommendation being considered.
2. Read the assigned primary sources. Extract the smallest exact passage
   that can support or limit each load-bearing claim. Give its source ID.
   Keep scope, population and dates attached to any empirical claim.
3. Form one specific, testable thesis and an ordered article outline.
   Name evidence that would change the thesis and record gaps in the
   evidence as limits. Keep an observation distinct from a causal inference.
4. Return the exact evidence alongside the proposed thesis and outline.
   Leave an inaccessible source or an unperformed calculation unresolved.
