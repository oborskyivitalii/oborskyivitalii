# Editorial workflow

Start in the owning issue. Record audience, language, purpose, desired outputs,
current stage and the exact version of any existing input. Use the
[parent session workflow](../../../guides/EDITORIAL-ORCHESTRATION.md#run-in-the-parent-session)
to select the next action and dispatch the appropriate
[editorial role](roles.md). Keep the artifact with its existing owner.

- **New article** — research, independent hypothesis check, author thesis
  decision, draft, independent manuscript check, author manuscript decision.
  Put the accepted material through the
  [existing article workflow](../../../guides/ARTICLE-WORKFLOW.md#authoring-and-publication-sequence).
- **Existing article or PDF** — start from the pinned edition and the actual
  change. For changed claims, repeat research and independent hypothesis
  review, then present the revised thesis for author approval. For substantive
  copy changes, obtain fresh manuscript review and author approval before
  rendering, and replace the affected downstream review IDs. For layout-only
  changes use the
  [existing rendition owner](../../../guides/EDITORIAL-ORCHESTRATION.md#existing-producers)
  and send only the changed output for author review.
- **Author-supplied draft** — pin the manuscript or post and the author's
  wording and position. Launch a fresh Editorial reviewer before manual edits.
  Retain `ready to sign`, `local edits` or `rewrite required` with its reasons.
  Preserve the author's changes and factual qualifiers. Attribute first-person
  experience only when the author supplied it.
- **Standalone talk** — apply research and independent hypothesis review to
  the talk brief. Ask for thesis and storyboard approval together. Draft slide
  copy and synchronized speaker notes. Launch a fresh Editorial reviewer on
  the slide copy and notes. Use the presentation owner for native export and
  validation, then collect the author's exact output decisions.
- **Distribution post** — start with the accepted edition and its claim
  qualifiers. Draft channel-specific copy and launch a fresh Editorial reviewer
  on that copy and edition, then request the author's decision on the exact post.
  For a post derived from an author-supplied manuscript, pin its first generated
  version and record the independent verdict and reasons before any manual edits.
  Verify the source manuscript hash before and after post-only corrections.
  Follow observed website and manual channel handoff in the
  [parent session workflow](../../../guides/EDITORIAL-ORCHESTRATION.md#existing-producers).

A request may use more than one route. Reuse the exact accepted input where
the requested outputs share it. Keep review decisions scoped to each output.
