# Systems thinking

Name the measurable variables, the direction of each supported link and any
delay. Identify a plausible reinforcing or balancing loop. If a queue or other
stock accumulates, distinguish inflow and outflow. Return the suspected
mechanism, an observation that could disprove it and what remains unknown.
