02 What we build

Uncertainty
Architecture

How do we design and operate software whose behavior depends on probabilistic Model Judgment? Uncertainty Architecture develops an engineering vocabulary and draft specification for Thinking Systems, AI control architecture, evaluation, decision authority, runtime control and correction.

Research themes

AI governance, architecture
& operating models.

The technical system and the human operating model have to work together. These are the questions behind my writing.

Thinking Systems & Model Judgment

In UA, Thinking Systems are identified by material runtime responsibilities that depend partly on probabilistic Model Judgment. The category is about an engineering condition, not consciousness or a guarantee of reliability.

Judgment Nodes locate where model judgment influences behavior; Uncertainty Boundaries connect deterministic responsibilities with that judgment.

Read the current UA vocabulary ↗

AI Control Plane & runtime control

AI control theory connects boundaries, evidence, decision authority and effective corrective action. UA's AI Control Plane is a distributed capability model across software, evaluation, release decisions and human workflows — not necessarily a central platform.

Explore the control capabilities ↗

Consequential Runtime Responsibility

Which model-mediated outputs, decisions or actions can materially affect outcomes, constraints, authority or downstream people and systems? That question helps connect AI architecture with evaluation, release evidence and accountability.

Socio-technical AI operating models

I study the socio-technical stack around AI: how team roles, review practices, decision rights, evaluation and incident ownership fit together. AI governance is not only a model setting; it also concerns how people operate and change the system.

05 / About

From delivery leadership
to AI architecture.

I have more than 20 years in IT, with a background spanning quality engineering, project management, PMO and delivery leadership. My practical work has included building delivery functions, resource planning, risk governance and organizational design.

That experience shapes my research: engineering decisions have consequences for teams, products and the people who own the outcome. I develop Uncertainty Architecture and The Subprime Code Crisis to examine AI architecture and governance, software verification, and the operating models needed around model-mediated behavior.

I write for architects, engineering leaders, product owners, delivery managers and PMO practitioners. This site connects the original articles, public talks and living research; Medium remains another place to read my work.
