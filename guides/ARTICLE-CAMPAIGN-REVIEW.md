# Manual campaign review at D+7 and D+21

Copy this template into a dated issue106 review record after the article actually
publishes. D0 is the observed site-publication time. Use the same structure for
each window; record the cutoff and elapsed age of every post. Empty or inaccessible
metrics are **unknown**, never zero. A planned review is not a reminder or schedule.

## Observation identity

- Article edition and actual canonical URL: pending.
- Actual D0 / review cutoff / timezone: pending.
- Window: D+7 or D+21; included publication URLs, timestamps and elapsed ages: pending.
- Available native/site sources, collection date and access limitations: unknown.
- Corrections, source changes, paused pieces or missing data: unknown.

| Aggregate | Observed value | Source and time | Meaning / limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Impressions / reach | unknown | unknown | Exposure, not readers; platform definitions differ. |
| Reads / article views | unknown | unknown | Record the source definition; do not equate to completion. |
| Saves / sends | unknown | unknown | Only available aggregates; never identify anonymous readers. |
| Substantive replies / useful examples | unknown | unknown | Count without names or private message text. |
| Substantive shares | unknown | unknown | A share is not a measured read or endorsement. |
| Site entries / referrers | unknown | unknown | Existing admitted aggregates only; unavailable routes remain unknown. |
| Author opportunities / follow-up conversations | unknown | unknown | Aggregate only, with no recipient or private correspondence. |

Compare equal elapsed windows and comparable formats. Note selection effects,
small samples, collection gaps and platform changes. A UTM label creates neither
collection nor causal attribution. Do not claim that the campaign caused a visit,
conversation or outcome without supporting evidence. A sent PDF is not a read.

## Editorial decision

- What was useful, and which public aggregate supports that assessment? unknown.
- What questions or misconceptions need a sourced clarification? unknown.
- Author reply capacity and next cadence: unknown.
- Next action: retain / reduce / revise / pause, with a concrete reason: pending.
- Material error: correct the published item, preserve history and re-review any
  affected unpublished derivative before continuing.
- Closing post: use only observed feedback. If no evidence exists, state that or
  omit the result; never fill the gap with invented engagement.

Channel rules, article release and actual publication/correction observations keep
their existing owners. This template grants no access, publication or rights decision.
