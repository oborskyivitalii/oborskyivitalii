# Site and publishing roadmap

This guide routes planned outcomes to their owning issues. Read those issues,
their linked PRs and [MEMORY](../MEMORY.md) for verified current work; this is
not a second status ledger. [README](../README.md) owns the project purpose and
[REPOSITORIES](REPOSITORIES.md) owns research and publication boundaries.

## Priority and acceptance routes

The maintainer's established sequence is the first useful personal site, then
the PMDay explanatory article. Later guides and reusable publishing/harness work
retain separate owners. A new direction is recorded in the relevant issue.

## Boundaries

A reversible site preview may proceed while production URL and rights decisions
are open. The first article needs a useful publication surface and its own
editorial/rights checks; a universal publisher, complete UA graph or cross-repo
harness is not an additional first-launch prerequisite.

Each publication keeps one editable owner and a separately identified accepted
edition. The site explains and links UA/Subprime research; it does not import
their scientific authority. PMDay transfer and Subprime adapter work remain
subject to their original owners and live review state.

Passing CI, merging one dependency, preparing a package and deploying an edition
are distinct outcomes. Completion is checked against the owning issue's current
acceptance criteria under [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
