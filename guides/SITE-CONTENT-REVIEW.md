# Editorial, edition and rights review

This guide defines the maintained review route for personal site content.
[SITE-SOURCE-AUDIT](SITE-SOURCE-AUDIT.md) owns claim-to-source and publication
provenance; [REPOSITORIES](REPOSITORIES.md) routes canonical UA/Subprime research.
The owning issue and linked PR hold the exact candidate and acceptance evidence.
Read [MEMORY](../MEMORY.md) and live GitHub records for current work.

## Review the exact edition

1. Identify the owning issue, candidate commit/tree, authored source and changed
   claims/editions. Edit [site sources](../site/README.md), then regenerate dependent
   public pages and review exports; generated pages are not editing owners.
2. Compare titles, author, actual publication/modified dates, URLs, language,
   format and rendition relationships with the original records. Keep published
   editions intact and later corrections separate. Drafts and prepared exports
   are not published editions.
3. Inspect affected public claims, author/profile context, attribution and links
   against [the source audit](SITE-SOURCE-AUDIT.md). Distinguish public statements,
   maintainer-attested provenance, source-reader limitations and weaker evidence.
   Link each material correction to its source and owning decision.
4. Verify generated HTML/PDF/deck output against the accepted source/manifest.
   Visually inspect applicable outputs and record actual desktop/mobile/browser
   coverage. A prior edition's review cannot accept changed bytes.
5. Record editorial, rights, independent/device and publication decisions as
   separate gates where required. Update both issue and PR with exact evidence,
   deviations and remaining work under [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Attribution and rights boundaries

- Index external publications using precise metadata and original links. The
  metadata inventory does not grant permission to import their article bodies,
  figures, logos, decks, fonts or private correspondence.
- Label actual English/Ukrainian editions EN/UA and use `en`/`uk` metadata.
  Do not invent translations or infer a talk's spoken language from its title.
- Describe public recognition within its source's scope: comment, reshare,
  recommendation, mapping or invited talk. A person's professional role does
  not establish institutional endorsement, co-authorship or research validation.
- Preserve the supplied portrait's provenance and third-party credits. Link
  bounded professional context to its public source; do not invent credentials,
  adoption metrics or quantified results.
- [Rights #7](https://github.com/oborskyivitalii/oborskyivitalii/issues/7) owns
  licensing and reuse decisions. Component-specific code notices do not license
  the whole site or its linked publications. Rights inventory is review evidence,
  not an automatic legal or publication approval.
- Semantic research corrections follow the owning UA/Subprime process. Site
  metadata, green CI and public discussion cannot promote their evidence states.
